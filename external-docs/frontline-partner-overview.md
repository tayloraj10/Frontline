# Frontline — Business Partnership Overview

*For Local Businesses*
*frontlinemaps.com*

## Turn neighborhood cleanup into foot traffic.

Frontline is a live map where New Yorkers claim territory and earn points for cleaning up their neighborhoods. Partner businesses put real rewards behind that effort — and get discovered by the people doing the work.

---

## What Frontline Is

Frontline runs Trash War, a city-wide cleanup campaign played out on a live map of New York City. Participants log the trash they collect — bags, pounds, cleaned blocks — and watch their neighborhood fill in as claimed territory. It's part game, part civic action, and it's active in neighborhoods across the city right now.

Every point earned is redeemable. That's where partner businesses come in.

---

## Why Businesses Partner

- **Reach people already in your neighborhood.** Participants are logging contributions blocks from your door — your pin on the map puts your offer in front of them exactly when they're nearby.
- **Pay only in what you already sell.** There's no partnership fee. You fund your own offer — a discount, a free item, whatever fits your business — and nothing more.
- **Stand for something visible.** Your business is named as a supporter of a cleanup effort your customers can see, claim, and talk about.
- **Stay in control.** You set the offer, the limits, and the timing — and you can change any of it whenever you want.

---

## How a Redemption Works

1. **You create an offer.** From your dashboard: a discount or a free item, how many times one person can redeem it, and how long it runs.
2. **Participants find it on the map.** Your business appears at its real location alongside the campaign, with your offer visible to anyone browsing nearby.
3. **A participant redeems in the app.** Once they've earned enough, they redeem and receive a unique code on their phone — nothing for you to look up.
4. **They show you the code in person.** You confirm it and mark the redemption used from your dashboard. Done.

> **No cost to join.** There's no fee to become a Frontline partner. You decide what to offer and when — you're only ever spending on the redemption itself, not on the partnership.

---

## Your Dashboard

- **Business profile** — Description, logo, hours, links, and your exact location on the campaign map.
- **Offers** — Create, edit, or retire offers any time — full control over what you're giving and how often.
- **Redemption confirmation** — Mark a redemption used the moment a customer shows you their code in store.
- **One login, self-service** — No approval needed for day-to-day changes — the dashboard is yours to run.
