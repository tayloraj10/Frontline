# Frontline — What You Can Do in the App

*A plain-language guide for three kinds of users: everyday participants, partner businesses, and admins.*
*Updated August 3, 2026*

---

## For Everyone Who Signs Up

### Getting started
You can create an account with email and password, or sign in instantly with Google. Once you're in, you can set up your profile with a display name, a short bio, and a profile photo. You can also manage your account security — change your password, change your email (unless you signed up through Google, in which case that's handled on Google's side), or permanently delete your account if you ever want to leave.

### Joining the action
Every campaign — like our flagship Trash War — plays out on a live map. You can browse the map and jump in by logging a contribution: drop a pin, log the bags or pounds you collected, snap a photo, and it's added to the board. If you're part of a group, you can claim territory on the group's behalf instead of your own — territory claiming is an optional layer you switch on from the map legend, not something shown by default. If you'd rather just see activity without any claiming/competition angle, you can instead switch on a stats view (by zip code, neighborhood, or borough) showing points, cleanup counts, and recent activity for that area.

You can also report a problem you spot — trash that needs cleaning up — by dropping a pin with a photo. Other users can then "claim" that report as a personal challenge: submit a before photo, do the cleanup, submit an after photo, and it automatically turns into a logged contribution.

If you ever come across an inappropriate photo anywhere in the app — a contribution photo on the map, an event gallery photo, or someone's profile picture — you can report it, and it's automatically taken down once enough people flag it.

### Cleanup events
Groups host real-world cleanup events you can RSVP to. You can check in when you arrive — either automatically via your phone's location or by entering a short code from the organizer — and see the event's roster, photos, and results. Depending on how the organizer set up the event, you might log your own contribution afterward, or the organizer will log the group's total for everyone.

### Groups
You're free to join or leave any group at any time, no approval needed. If you'd rather start your own group, you can apply — an admin reviews the application, and if it's approved, you become that group's first admin automatically.

### Partner rewards
As you rack up points, you can redeem them for real perks at partner businesses — think discounts or freebies from local shops supporting the cause. Redeeming gives you a unique code to show the business. If you run a business yourself and want to offer something back to the community, you can apply to become a partner.

### Your profile and the leaderboard
Anyone can view your public profile and your activity history, and there's a global leaderboard showing top contributors.

## For Group Organizers

If you're an admin of a group, you get extra tools:

- Edit your group's profile, logo, and social links.
- Manage your member list — promote other members to admin, or remove someone if needed.
- Host a cleanup event: set the time and place, decide whether attendees log their own contributions or you log one combined total for the group, and generate a join code so people can check in without needing GPS.
- Run the event day-of: manually check people in, add someone who shows up unannounced, and log contributions on behalf of attendees who can't do it themselves.
- After the event, log the team's total haul and the app automatically splits the points fairly across everyone who participated (you can also fine-tune individual shares if needed).
- If your group ever needs to be shut down, you can delete it — the app will stop you if there are still active events tied to it, so nothing falls through the cracks.

## For Partner Businesses

If your business partners with us, you get a dashboard to manage everything yourself:

- Update your business profile — description, logo, hours, location on the map, and links.
- Create and manage the offers you want to provide to participants (a discount, a free item, whatever makes sense for your business), including how many times each person can redeem it.
- When a customer redeems an offer in person, you can mark it as used right from your dashboard.

Right now, business accounts are focused specifically on our Trash War campaign, since that's the one currently active on the map.

## For Admins

Admins have full oversight of the platform through a dedicated admin panel:

- **Users** — search and manage user accounts.
- **Campaigns** — create and configure campaigns, and control which ones count toward redeemable points. Any change that affects users' point balances shows a preview of exactly who's affected before it's confirmed.
- **Events** — set up special time-limited campaign events (like bonus-point challenges in a specific area).
- **Partner businesses** — review and approve new business applications, and manage their offers.
- **Groups** — review and approve new group applications.
- **Leaderboard** — verify top contributors for prize eligibility.

Admins can also step onto the live map to create special timed events by picking an area directly, and they automatically have the same tools available to group organizers and partner businesses, in case they ever need to step in and help.

## A Few Things Still In Progress

We like to be upfront about what's still a work in progress:

- Some of our "dynamic event" effects (special bonuses tied to real-world milestones) are wired up on the backend but don't yet do anything visible when they fire — this is active development, not a bug.
- Cleanup event participation is currently honor-system when it comes to location — we trust people to log honestly rather than strictly enforcing GPS checks on every submission.
- Business accounts can currently only have one manager at a time; multi-manager support isn't built yet.
- Our updated Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are ready, but the pop-up that asks existing users to formally re-accept them is temporarily turned off while we finish rolling it out.
