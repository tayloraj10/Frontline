Payments revamp mocks

Static design mocks for priority 2 (2026-10-22): the paid cleaner marketplace plus a revamp of the Rewards cards, Sponsorships page and Impact page. Mobile-first, dark theme, same colors as the app (emerald = earn, sky = impact, amber = Plus, violet = admin). Fake data. Nothing here is built yet. Notes under each section list the proposed changes and open questions.

1. Rewards page: the two cards

Today: two small cards ("Sponsored cleanups", "Impact") above the partner perks. They say nothing about what is inside, and Impact is a stat page, not an action. Proposal: one wide "Cash and funding" card with live numbers and two clear actions, so the two jobs (earn real money, see impact) are obvious. Visible to admins only for now.

Money model shown: hold at acceptance, release on confirm, fee on top for the customer (Rover charges both sides, so this is a placeholder). Shared design decisions to confirm

  • Segmented tabs (Rewards, Sponsorships, Impact, Hire) instead of pill rows of features.
  • Money amounts are always large, right-aligned, emerald for earnings and amber for held funds.
  • Sticky bottom action bar on forms (mobile thumb reach).
  • Everything stays admin-gated (payments gate plus a new marketplace_* flag) until legal, tax and Connect are done.
  • No percentages, impact claims or fee numbers in real UI until the data can back them.
